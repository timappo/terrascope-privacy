Support

When reporting an issue, it helps to include your iOS version and device model, a short description of what happened versus what you expected, and — if it's about a specific world — roughly how large it is.

Where does TerraScope get my world files?

You pick a .wld file yourself using the iOS file picker. TerraScope only reads files you explicitly choose.

Does it upload my worlds anywhere?

No. Everything is parsed and rendered locally on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

Which Terraria version is supported?

Terraria 1.4.5.x world files (format version v319). Older formats may not load.

The minimap looks blank or says "too large to render."

Very large worlds are capped for memory safety. Try reloading the world, or reopening the app if it has been in the background for a long time.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2026

No Data Collection

TerraScope does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Your Terraria world files (.wld) are read entirely on your device and are never uploaded to any server.

No Analytics or Tracking

TerraScope contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking of any kind.

No Accounts Required

TerraScope does not require or support user accounts, logins, or sign-ups.

File Access

TerraScope requests access to files solely to open Terraria world files you explicitly select. No files are read without your action, and no file contents leave your device.

Privacy of Minors

TerraScope does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

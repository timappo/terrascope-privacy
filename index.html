TerraScope Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 31, 2026

No Data Collection

TerraScope does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Your Terraria world files (.wld) are read entirely on your device and are never uploaded to any server.

No Analytics or Tracking

TerraScope contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking of any kind.

No Accounts Required

TerraScope does not require or support user accounts, logins, or sign-ups.

File Access

TerraScope requests access to files solely to open Terraria world files you explicitly select. No files are read without your action, and no file contents leave your device.

Privacy of Minors

TerraScope does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

Any future changes to this policy will be posted on this page with an updated date.
